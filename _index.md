I am a PhD candidate in Business Technology Management at the [Haskayne School of Business](https://haskayne.ucalgary.ca/), University of Calgary, affiliated with the [Informatics Research Centre (iRC)](https://haskayne.ucalgary.ca/research/informatics-research-centre). My research sits at the intersection of digital platform economics, behavioral economics, and data privacy.

My dissertation examines how different monetization models shape user behavior and welfare across digital platforms — from blockchain-based incentive systems in gaming to consent-based monetization under privacy regulation. I am supervised by [Dr. Hooman Hidaji](https://haskayne.ucalgary.ca/profiles/hooman-hidaji).

I joined the PhD program in Fall 2021, bringing a background from my master's degree in Iran and a deep interest in understanding why people respond the way they do under different system designs.
